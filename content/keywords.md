## A warning about keyword lists

Lists that come out of keyword tools are full of high-volume heads — "email
marketing", "customer retention". A domain a few months old will not rank for
those for years; those results belong to industry publications with thousands of
links pointing at them.

What is winnable now is the long question: "why do subscribers churn in the
second month", "how to measure retention impact honestly". Lower volume, but
they are won by an article rather than by a domain — and they are the phrasing
AI search engines quote.
